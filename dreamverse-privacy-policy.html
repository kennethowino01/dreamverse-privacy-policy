Dreamverse Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 24, 2026

Dreamverse ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Dreamverse mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information from users:

  • Email address (for account creation and login)
  • Profile image (optional)
  • Credit purchase and usage history within the app

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as payment card details, social security numbers, or contacts beyond what you explicitly provide.

Permissions

The Dreamverse app requests certain permissions on your device for specific features. These include:

  • Blocked permissions to protect user privacy: RECORD_AUDIO, CAMERA, MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS, READ_CONTACTS, WRITE_CONTACTS, READ_CALENDAR, WRITE_CALENDAR, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, READ_PHONE_STATE, CALL_PHONE, READ_CALL_LOG, WRITE_CALL_LOG, SEND_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS, READ_SMS
  • Required permissions for app features: com.android.vending.BILLING (for in-app credit purchases)

We do not share any user data collected with third parties, except as necessary to process in-app purchases via secure payment systems.

Use of Information

The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing your Dreamverse account
  • Enabling credit purchases and dream interpretation services
  • Improving the App’s features and user experience
  • Communicating updates or account-related information

Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. Data is securely stored and protected according to industry best practices.

Children's Privacy

Dreamverse is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.
