# Dreamverse Privacy Policy
Effective Date: January 24, 2026
Dreamverse (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use Dreamverse, available on the Google Play Store. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.
________________________________________
1. Information We Collect
When you use Dreamverse, we may collect the following types of information:
1.1 Account Information
•	Email address (used to create and manage your account).
•	Optional profile image (if you choose to upload one).
1.2 In-App Transactions
•	Payment information is processed exclusively by Google Play Billing and RevenueCat.
•	We do not store or access your credit card information.
1.3 Device Permissions
Dreamverse blocks unnecessary permissions by default, but may request the following system permissions for optional features:
•	Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) – Used only if you record dreams for interpretation.
•	Camera (CAMERA) – Used only if you choose to upload profile photos or dream-related images.
•	Audio settings (MODIFY_AUDIO_SETTINGS) – For proper audio recording/playback during dream recording.
All other sensitive permissions like contacts, calendar, location, call logs, SMS, or phone state are blocked and not accessed by the App.
________________________________________
2. How We Use Your Information
We use the information collected for the following purposes:
1.	Account Management: To create and manage your user account.
2.	App Functionality: To provide dream interpretation services and manage credit balances.
3.	Customer Support: To assist you with account issues or in-app purchases.
We do not sell, share, or distribute your personal information to any third party.
________________________________________
3. In-App Purchases
Dreamverse uses RevenueCat and Google Play Billing to handle in-app purchases. Your purchase data is managed securely through these platforms, and Dreamverse does not directly handle your payment information.
________________________________________
4. Data Storage and Security
•	Personal information is stored securely in our database.
•	We implement reasonable technical measures to protect your data against unauthorized access or disclosure.
________________________________________
5. User Rights
•	You can update your email or profile picture at any time within the app.
•	You may request account deletion, which removes all your personal data from Dreamverse servers.
________________________________________
6. Children’s Privacy
Dreamverse is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware of such data, it will be deleted immediately.
________________________________________
7. Updates to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. All updates will be posted within the App and on the website hosting this policy.
