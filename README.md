Dreamverse Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 24, 2026

Dreamverse ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the Dreamverse mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: Email address and profile image (if uploaded).

Financial Information: Purchase history and subscription status (processed via Google Play Billing and RevenueCat). We do not store credit card details.

Media & Files: If you use the dream interpretation or profile features, we may request access to your Photos and Media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES) to allow you to upload images.

App Performance Data: We may collect technical logs, such as app load times and crash reports, to ensure the AI agents are responding correctly and to improve app stability.

App Interactions: Dreamverse collects app interaction data (such as feature usage and AI agent engagement) to perform internal analytics and improve the quality of our dream interpretations. This data is used solely by the developer to study user behavior and enhance app functionality; it is not shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

2. Use of Information

The information we collect is used for the following purposes:

Creating and managing your Dreamverse account

Enabling credit purchases and dream interpretation services

Improving the App’s features and user experience

Communicating updates or account-related information

3. Device Permissions

To provide our services, Dreamverse may request the following permissions:

Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Only used if you choose to record your dreams via voice

Photos/Media (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Only used to upload images for interpretation or profile customization

In-App Purchases (BILLING): To process subscriptions and credit refills

4. Third-Party Services

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and Google Play Services for app functionality and billing. These services may collect data as governed by their respective privacy policies.

5. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties. Data is securely stored and protected according to industry best practices.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data as long as your account is active. You have the right to delete your data. You can request account and data deletion at any time by:

Emailing us at dreamverse37@gmail.com

Using the "Delete Account" feature within the App settings

7. Children's Privacy

Dreamverse is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We do not sell your personal data. All data is encrypted in transit using standard industry protocols.
